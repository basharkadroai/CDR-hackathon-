## One-Sentence Pitch

DealVault turns confidential deal documents into programmable on-chain objects:
encrypted in the browser, protected by CDR, and unlocked only when smart-contract
conditions like wallet allowlists, deadlines, multi-sig approvals, or escrow
payments pass.
