Hey everyone! 👋

Just deployed DealVault for the CDR Hackathon: https://dealvault-sable.vercel.app

It's a trustless document vault with two modes:
• Deal Room: Time-limited sharing (like traditional VDRs but on-chain)
• Dead Drop: Sealed docs that unlock on a future date

Built with CDR SDK, running on Story Testnet in mock mode.

Would love feedback! 🙏
