### Technical Deep Dive (Post #5)
```
How DealVault works under the hood:

1. File encrypted client-side
2. Uploaded to CDR vault on @StoryProtocol
3. Condition contract deployed:
   - Time window check
   - Wallet allowlist
   - Future unlock trigger

4. Recipient connects wallet → conditions checked on-chain → vault decrypts

Zero trust. Pure crypto.
```

## 🎥 LinkedIn Post

```
🚀 Excited to share DealVault — a project I built for the Story Protocol CDR Hackathon

**The Problem:**
Virtual data rooms (VDRs) for fundraising and M&A cost $99-$25,000/month and require trusting a centralized company.

**The Solution:**
DealVault uses blockchain smart contracts to enforce access control. No servers. No middlemen. Just cryptographic guarantees.

**Two Modes:**

📁 Deal Room
Perfect for founders raising capital or companies in M&A. Upload confidential documents, set authorized wallet addresses, and define an access window. When the window closes, access revokes automatically on-chain.

🔒 Dead Drop
A genuinely novel feature: sealed documents that unlock on a future date. Nobody can open them—including the creator—until the smart contract condition is met. Ideal for succession plans, wills, and time-locked releases.

**Tech Stack:**
Built on Story Protocol's Confidential Data Rails (CDR) with Next.js, TypeScript, and deployed on Vercel.

**Try it:** https://dealvault-sable.vercel.app

Submission deadline is June 3. Would love your feedback!

#Web3 #Blockchain #Startup #Fundraising #LegalTech
```
